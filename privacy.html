Privacy

How this website handles browser data and third-party video services.

Overview

JixeGo Live is a static website. It does not provide user accounts, operate a database, or intentionally collect personal information through this site.

YouTube data

The site requests public channel and video information from YouTube Data API v3, including channel branding, public statistics, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, durations, categories, and public video statistics. These requests are subject to the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

Local storage

To improve speed and reduce API requests, the browser may temporarily store a cached copy of public YouTube channel data. This cache contains no account credentials or viewer profile information and expires automatically.

Embedded videos

Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain. YouTube may still process information when a visitor chooses to play a video. Visitors can watch directly on YouTube instead by using each video's external link.

External links

This site links to YouTube and Google services. Their privacy practices apply after leaving this website.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when site features or third-party services change. The version displayed here is effective July 27, 2026.
